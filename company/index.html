Our game

We develop and publish Vonkelveld, a simulation game about shaping a living world and following the people who inhabit it. The Windows edition is available on Steam in Early Access. An iPad edition is in preparation, with two free curated worlds and an optional permanent full-game purchase. It is not yet available on the App Store.

Software MoosS (Pty) Ltd is a company registered in South Africa with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC).
