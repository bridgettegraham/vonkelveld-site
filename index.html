What it is

You are a force, not a king

Vonkelveld — Afrikaans for spark field — is a god-sim in which every light scattered across the dark veld is a simulated life. You cannot order anyone to do anything. You shape conditions: raise land and sink it, call the rain, warm the air, bless the ground, bring ruin. Then time runs, and the people decide for themselves where to settle, when to flee, and who to follow.

Fall from a god's eye to a single life

Pull back and hearth-lights bloom into villages, then cities with real streets. Zoom out further and the map closes into a torus. Dive the other way and follow one person through their whole life — their family, their needs, their death.

Ask why, and get the truth

Point at any place and ask why here. The game answers with the actual causal chain that produced it, traced back through the years — your acts, the climate, migration, collapse, chance. Not flavour text.

Deterministic to the byte

A world is nothing but a seed plus your choices. A seed and the same sequence of choices reproduce the same simulation. Share a seed to compare how differently you each guide its people.

Drawn from nothing but arithmetic

The map, the weather, the day and night, every sound — all generated at runtime from hand-authored primitive recipes. No stock art. No AI-generated imagery. A world that looks and sounds like only itself.

The worlds

Eleven worlds to explore

The cold, the flood, the fire — each scenario's pressure is different, so each plays completely differently. A perfect world is not promised. Skill is what your hand preserves, and what it learns to let go.
